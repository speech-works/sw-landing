## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A practice app for adults who stutter: rehearse the conversations that feel hard before you're in them.
**What it does:** Guided programs (7 to 14 days) for specific situations: job interviews, phone calls, telling people you stutter, dating, and the worry before and after speaking. Interview and phone call programs include practice calls with an AI character that is built to wait while you stutter. Free daily practice sits alongside the programs: reading, breathing, role plays, and one short AI call a week.
**Product category:** Speaking practice app for people who stutter (stammer). People search for it as a "stammering app", "stuttering app", "how to stop stammering" *(assumption; see voc.md)*.
**Product type:** Mobile app (Android live on Google Play; iOS status unclear: the site says "coming soon", the internal listing doc is for the App Store).
**Business model:** Free to download. Programs are one-time purchases, owned forever: 8 programs at ₹999, the 2 AI-call programs (interview, phone) at ₹1,999. Launch offer halves these (₹499 / ₹999) while `LAUNCH_OFFER_ACTIVE` is on; confirm the production setting before quoting a price. Optional membership (monthly or yearly) unlocks the full technique videos and a monthly allowance of AI call credits. Call credits can also be bought on their own.

## Target Audience
**Who:** Adults who stutter, mainly in India, who read English. Students heading into campus placements and early-career professionals first *(assumption)*.
**Decision-maker:** The person who stutters, paying out of their own pocket.
**Primary use case:** Getting ready for a specific speaking situation they're dreading.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me get through the interview / call coming up without avoiding it."
- "Give me somewhere private to practice saying things out loud."
- "Help me decide whether and how to tell people I stutter."
**Use cases:** campus or job interview, calling a bank or a repair shop, introducing yourself and saying your name, telling a manager or a date, recovering after a bad block.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Hard speaking moments come up in real life with no chance to rehearse, so people avoid them, swap words, or stay silent.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Speech therapy: expensive, scarce outside metros, and usually weekly sessions, not practice for tomorrow's interview.
- YouTube and free tips: generic, no practice partner, often promise a "cure".
- Fluency apps: built around fluency scores and "cure" framing that many people who stutter reject; mostly subscriptions.
- Practising with friends or family: embarrassing, not always available.
**What it costs them:** job offers, promotions, and phone tasks passed to others. Avoiding situations and swapping words, which is tiring.
**Emotional tension:** dread before speaking, shame after a block, fear of being judged or laughed at.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Stamurai (Gurgaon; subscription, US $19.99/week; "Increase fluency"; reviews complain about billing and generic exercises). StutterLab ($99/mo, AI practice, US). Stutter Coach (iOS, "fluency metrics"). All subscriptions, all fluency-framed, none India-priced with AI call practice.
**India cure/clinic players:** Ankush Pare ("cure code… guaranteed result in 28 days"), indianspeechtherapy.in ("Stammering Treatment in India"), Sounderic (online SLP therapy). Hide prices, promise cure or fluency.
**Secondary:** Speech therapists, and self-help groups such as TISA (The Indian Stammering Association). Fall short: limited availability, and no rehearsal for a specific situation on demand.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing and avoiding (the real status quo). General interview-prep and English-speaking apps, which aren't built for someone who stutters.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Practice for real situations (interview, phone call, disclosure), not generic drills.
- An AI caller built to give you time, including when you stutter.
- No fluency score, no streaks, no leaderboard. Progress counts what you did, not how you sounded.
- Pay once for a program and keep it. Missed days never go "behind".
- Private: the Mirror camera video never leaves the phone.
**Why that's better:** You can rehearse the exact moment you're dreading, as often as you want, without being graded.

**Anti-persona:** Parents looking for help for young children. Clinicians looking for assessment tools. Anyone who wants a fluency score or a guaranteed result.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** an interview or call is coming up; last time went badly.
**Pull:** private rehearsal of that exact situation; no judgement; one-off price.
**Habit:** avoiding the situation, swapping words, letting others speak for them.
**Anxiety:** "It won't work", "I'll waste money", "I'll feel embarrassed even talking to an AI".

## Customer Language
Full list: `docs/copy-validation/voc.md`. Indians say **stammer** (TISA, Hindi *haklana*), not stutter.
**How they describe the problem:**
- "I am NOT getting placement due to stammering"
- "Every phone call was a test."
- "I was rehearsing to fail, creating an internal movie of my own failure."
- "i couldn't sleep yesterday night because of the fear"
- changing a word mid-sentence ("Butter"), pointing, skipping the fresher's welcome
**What they want:** "I can then easily say whatever u want to say"; "Stammer as much as you want, just please don't stop speaking." 
**Words to use:** stammer first, stutter once in brackets; placement, practice, prepare, say it out loud, interview, phone call, your own words, at your pace.
**Words to avoid:** cure, fix, treatment, therapy, clinical, fluency, stutterer, overcome, battle, "just relax", any promised result, suffer/sufferer, "slow down". Honest negations are fine ("no fluency score", "doesn't treat stammering").
