Keep the copy honest

  • AI calls belong to two named programs. Do not imply every program has them.
  • The new avatar artwork is a website preview. Avoid claiming this exact wardrobe is already in the app.
  • The iOS status shown here matches the current website; confirm it before publication.
  • About copy needs verified people and authorship details before it can tell a founder story.

This is a copy review based on the current source and program catalogue. It does not measure conversion or determine who wrote the existing text.

Shared practical copy: FAQ and app preview labels

What happens in a program?

You work through daily lessons and activities over 7 to 14 days. The outline on each program page shows what you will do.

Does every program include AI calls?

Interview Ready and The Hard Conversations include AI call practice and bundled credits. Other programs use activities such as writing and speaking practice.

Do I need a subscription?

Programs are one-time purchases. Optional membership and extra AI call credits are separate. The app shows the price before you buy.

On the stacked cards

  • "Your next chapter" becomes "14-day program". Remove the duplicate duration below it.
  • "A plan. Some practice. Your next step." becomes "See the daily outline".
  • "A little room to practise" becomes "Breathing practice".
  • "Your words, to keep" becomes "Saved notes".

Keep the same factual labels across all four directions. Each card needs to explain its feature.
